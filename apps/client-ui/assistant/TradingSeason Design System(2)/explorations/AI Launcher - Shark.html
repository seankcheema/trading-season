Notes

Round 3 (3a–3f) iterates on 1a: same line drawing, each with one added idea. Round 2 (2a–2c) adds character: two-tone body, big glossy eyes, blush, a one-tooth grin and fins that gesture. Rounds 2 and 1 are kept below for comparison. All are original drawings, not Jeff the Land Shark (a Marvel character). They borrow the same feel: round, small, friendly, a little goofy. The style follows Notion's illustration approach: one stroke weight (2.5 on a 64 grid), round caps, dot eyes, flat fills, no gradients.

Hover: the button lifts 2px, the fin wiggles and the eyes blink once. Colorways: frost (stands out on the dark app; recommended), ink with hairline (quiet), cyan (on brand, but competes with the primary buttons).

These are hand-built vector sketches. If you go ahead, an illustrator should redraw the final mascot.
