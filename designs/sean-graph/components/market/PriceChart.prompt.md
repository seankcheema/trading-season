Interactive line chart for portfolio value and instrument prices; color follows the period's direction.

```jsx
<PriceChart area points={series} labels={['9:30 AM', '11:00 AM', '12:30 PM', '2:00 PM', '3:30 PM']} height={260} />
```

Hovering shows "$316.59 +1.24% · 2:15 PM" above the plot. Axis labels in `$` whole dollars at 11px.
